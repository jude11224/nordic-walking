Meet Julie

Do you worry about falling? I do.

It is so easy to trip or lose your balance. I'm 74 years-old and have successfully worked as a fitness instructor and personal trainer for older adults for the past 18 years. I understand your concerns and needs.

I'll help you improve your balance as well as your posture and gait, and build the confidence to keep living the way you want to.

Fitness offerings

Classes built for a strong, steady life

See all offerings →
  1. 01

    Stretch and Strengthen

    One hour · Tuesdays & Fridays, 10:00 · LaGrua Center, Stonington

    Designed to assist older adults in maintaining their lifestyle and independence. By increasing strength and flexibility, you also improve your balance. Class includes use of small hand weights, bands, and steppers.

  2. 02

    Balance, Posture, Gait

    One hour · Individual or small group · 3-month class

    A class to improve your balance as well as your posture and gait. No pills. No tricks. No serums. Just strategies, exercises and improvements. Look better, feel better, regain your confidence.

    About this class →
  3. 03

    Nordic Walking

    7-week class · YMCA, Arcadia Branch

    A full-body exercise to improve posture, upper body strength, cardiovascular health and burn more calories than with regular walking. Learn and practice a sport enjoyed by adults around the world, including training on walking local trails.

    About Nordic Walking →
  4. 04

    Personal Training

    One-hour individual session · LaGrua or Forget Me Not

    Addresses any areas of weakness while enhancing strengths to promote progression and avoid a plateau. Draws from the three classes above to improve agility, neuro-muscular connection, and reaction time to prevent falls and boost performance. Special focus on feet, ankles, wrists and hands available.
